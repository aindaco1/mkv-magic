# MKV Magic 0.1.8 beta

MKV Magic 0.1.8 introduces its production app icon, fixes removal of track
statistics tags reported by MKVToolNix 101, and reduces duplicated execution
plumbing without changing the app's verified-output behavior.

## Highlights

- The Universal app and DMG now carry MKV Magic's black-and-white Nested
  Container icon at every standard macOS representation from 16 px through
  1024 px. Source and packaged-app gates validate the master PNG and ICNS.
- Tag inspection recognizes MKVToolNix 101 files that flatten statistics such
  as BPS, DURATION, NUMBER_OF_FRAMES, and NUMBER_OF_BYTES into per-track
  properties instead of listing them in the top-level track-tag summary.
- Tag review uses the exact bounded XML extracted by `mkvextract` as its final
  count and identity authority. Removal still edits only a clone, then proves
  that every global and track tag is absent before and after commit.
- Shared construction now supplies the bundled tool catalog, command runner,
  and media inspector to every app workflow. A single bounded diagnostic policy
  also replaces twelve copies of external-tool error formatting.

## Encoding and compatibility

- Requires macOS 13 Ventura or newer and ships as one Universal app for Apple
  Silicon and Intel Macs.
- Bundles MKVToolNix 101.0 and FFmpeg 9.0.1. The tag fix specifically covers
  MKVToolNix 101's flattened per-track statistics representation.
- Tag removal, metadata editing, and compatible joins remain zero-encode
  operations. Video or audio is encoded only when the reviewed operation
  explicitly requires conversion.

## Safety and privacy

- Originals are never overwritten. Tag removal operates on a clone, verifies
  unchanged tracks, title, chapters, attachments, and segment identity, commits
  to a new path, and reopens the result for a final audit.
- MKV Magic runs locally, with no accounts, telemetry, uploads, remote APIs, or
  LLM dependency. Update checks occur only when explicitly requested.

## Current limitations

- Image-subtitle OCR is not included.
- Workflows that add an external subtitle remain single-file because each
  video/subtitle pairing requires explicit review.
- HDR10+, HLG, Dolby Vision, SDR-to-HDR conversion, ordered-edition trims,
  background folder watching, crossfades, and transitions are not included.

## Requirements

- macOS 13 Ventura or newer on Apple Silicon or Intel.
- Enough free disk space for the verified output and temporary work.
- User-selected read/write access to every source and automatic destination.

## Candidate acceptance

This candidate remains a draft until its exact DMG passes clean-account Apple
Silicon and physical Intel installation checks, the prior-version Sparkle
replacement check, and real-file playback verification in Jellyfin/Plex.
