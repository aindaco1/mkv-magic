# MKV Magic 0.1.1 beta

MKV Magic is a local, lossless-first macOS utility for inspecting and safely
changing Matroska media. This first public-beta candidate focuses on common
Jellyfin and Plex library work while preserving every source until a new output
has been reopened and verified.

## Highlights

- Inspect video, audio, subtitle, chapter, tag, and attachment facts with a
  bundled Universal FFmpeg and MKVToolNix runtime.
- Edit titles and track metadata without encoding, remove selected tracks or
  attachments through verified remuxes, and export or clear Matroska tags.
- Add external SRT, ASS, or SSA subtitles; extract embedded text subtitles; and
  review deterministic advertisement, whitespace, encoding, and English OCR
  cleanup without an LLM or network service.
- Create and edit nested Matroska chapters, generate interval chapters, review
  local scene, black-frame, and silence suggestions, and intelligently rebase
  chapters while joining files.
- Join compatible MKVs without encoding, fast-trim at disclosed keyframes, or
  use exact numeric in/out points with one reviewed conversion generation.
- Convert eligible complete files to MKV with AV1, HEVC, H.264, or ProRes while
  packet-copying compatible audio and preserving supported HDR10 metadata.
- Build portable plain-language workflows, review their exact per-file impact,
  fuse compatible conversion choices into one generation, and run them now or
  through the durable resource-aware queue.
- Review privacy-safe local History, retry or recover work, and optionally move
  a source to Trash only after verified success.

## Encoding and compatibility

- Requires macOS 13 Ventura or newer and ships as one Universal app for Apple
  Silicon and Intel Macs.
- MKV is the default output and packet copy is preferred. Software AV1 is the
  preferred quality-and-size conversion when its local capability probe passes;
  HEVC VideoToolbox is the faster fallback for older or slower Intel hardware.
- Audio is preserved by default. Explicit AAC, Opus, AC-3, E-AC-3, or FLAC
  conversion is offered only when the bundled runtime can preserve the reviewed
  channel-layout and sample-rate contract.

## Safety and privacy

- Originals are never overwritten. Media results are created in private
  temporary storage, semantically audited, committed to a new path, reopened,
  and audited again.
- MKV Magic runs locally without accounts, telemetry, media uploads, remote
  APIs, LLMs, or a Homebrew dependency. The only shipped network path is the
  explicit Check for Updates command through Sparkle's sandboxed helper.
- Saved workflows contain portable intent rather than media paths, track IDs,
  subtitle text, or per-file review choices.

## Current limitations

- Image-subtitle OCR is not included. PGS and VobSub may be preserved or
  removed, but are not converted to text.
- Attachment extraction and tag export/removal are direct single-file actions;
  attachment replacement and selective tag editing are not included yet.
- Standalone common-container conversion intentionally refuses ambiguous data,
  subtitle, chapter, color, or multi-video layouts instead of guessing. MKV,
  zero-encode remux, and the separate TX3G-to-ASS action cover the supported
  bounded alternatives.
- HDR10+, HLG, Dolby Vision, SDR-to-HDR conversion, ordered-edition trims,
  background folder watching, automatic update installation, crossfades, and
  transitions are not included.

## Requirements

- macOS 13 Ventura or newer.
- Enough free disk space for a verified new output and temporary work.
- User-selected read/write access to every source and destination.
