# MKV Magic 0.1.5 beta

MKV Magic is a local, lossless-first macOS utility for inspecting and safely
changing Matroska media. This beta adds a ready-to-use cleanup workflow modeled
on the project's original Python cleaner.

## Highlights

- Open Workflows and use the built-in **Clean MKV** recipe immediately. It
  removes explicitly non-English subtitles and redundant English SDH tracks,
  while preserving the sole useful English or unknown-language subtitle.
- Remove the segment title, all Matroska tags, and reviewed image attachments;
  subtitle fonts and attachments with unknown MIME types remain intact.
- Normalize recognized commentary track names and flags, mark recognized forced
  and SDH subtitles, and offer a conservative Jellyfin/Plex-friendly filename.
- Preview every applicable and skipped step before running. The workflow edits
  portable intent, so its cards can be disabled, reordered, removed, duplicated,
  exported, or extended like any other saved workflow.
- Create a new workflow from the same cleanup cards with fresh identifiers.
  Saving an empty workflow library is respected and does not restore the preset.

## Encoding and compatibility

- Requires macOS 13 Ventura or newer and ships as one Universal app for Apple
  Silicon and Intel Macs.
- The Clean MKV workflow performs no audio or video encoding. It uses one
  packet-copy remux and at most one property edit when the reviewed file needs
  them, then verifies the complete output.
- MKV remains the default output, and packet copy or metadata editing is used
  whenever the reviewed change does not require transcoding.
- Software AV1 remains the preferred quality-and-size conversion for explicit
  transcoding when its local capability probe passes. HEVC VideoToolbox is the
  faster fallback for older or slower Intel hardware.
- Audio and video tracks are preserved by default. A separate conversion card
  is required before the workflow planner can encode either stream type.

## Safety and privacy

- Originals are never overwritten. Outputs are built in private temporary
  storage, verified, committed to a new path, reopened, and verified again.
- The output directory defaults to the original file's directory, with the
  standard save panel available to choose another directory. Moving an original
  to Trash remains a separate opt-in that occurs only after verified success.
- Steps that do not apply are reported as skipped. A combined plan cannot issue
  a metadata edit against a subtitle track it has selected for removal.
- MKV Magic runs locally without accounts, telemetry, media uploads, remote
  APIs, or LLMs. The only shipped network path is the explicit Check for Updates
  command through Sparkle's sandboxed helper.

## Current limitations

- Image-subtitle OCR is not included. PGS and VobSub may be preserved or
  removed, but are not converted to text.
- Subtitle-language cleanup relies on inspected Matroska language metadata.
  Unknown-language subtitles are preserved for review.
- The built-in recipe intentionally does not reproduce the legacy script's
  aggressive behavior of disabling every subtitle when English audio exists,
  forcing every subtitle when it does not, or deleting unknown attachments.
- HDR10+, HLG, Dolby Vision, SDR-to-HDR conversion, ordered-edition trims,
  background folder watching, crossfades, and transitions are not included.

## Requirements

- macOS 13 Ventura or newer on Apple Silicon or Intel.
- Enough free disk space for a verified new output and temporary work.
- User-selected read/write access to every source and destination.

Publication remains separate from candidate creation and requires clean-account
Apple Silicon and Intel acceptance plus prior-version Sparkle replacement
against the exact candidate DMG digest.
